The monitor separates observed political interaction from policy-interest concentration and voting alignment. It is designed to identify changing country groups without treating simple co-occurrence as political agreement.

Interpretation rule: RSS/GDELT links show observed interaction and topic co-occurrence. Only voting data should be interpreted as voting alignment. Composite alignment will be added after the underlying layers are validated.
